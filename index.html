The Problem

Horse owners, trainers, and vets struggle to monitor key vitals and movement patterns in real-time — risking injury, stress, or undetected issues. Most current systems are invasive, expensive, or hard to use.

🐴 Lack of Real-Time Health Monitoring

Lack of continuous heart rate, oxygen, or temperature data makes early issue detection hard.

⏰ Reactive, Not Preventive Care

Most issues are caught after symptoms appear, costing time, money, and sometimes the horse’s well-being.

🧠 Lack of Smart Analytics

Most trackers don’t offer AI-based insights or early alerts for health and performance.

What EquiKai Delivers

A sleek, non-invasive device that mounts under the girth strap and tracks your horse’s vitals, movement, and location — all powered by AI.

Heart & Oxygen Monitoring

Continuously monitor heart rate and SpO₂ for early signs of stress or fatigue.

Live GPS Tracking

Know your horse’s exact location at all times, whether on the farm or during transport.

AI-Powered Insights

Our backend gives you personalized alerts, performance metrics, and health forecasts.
